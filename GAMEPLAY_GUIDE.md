# 🏁 Rollercoaster Race - Gameplay Guide

## Getting Started

### First Time Players

When you first join the Rollercoaster Race world, here's what to expect:

1. **Welcome Message** - You'll receive a chat message welcoming you to the race
2. **Type `/race`** - This opens the interactive menu system
3. **Choose "How to Play"** - Read the instructions if you're new
4. **Select Your Track** - Pick from 5 colorful racing tracks
5. **Get Ready** - Position yourself at the starting line
6. **Race!** - Wait for the countdown and GO!

### Quick Start Commands

- **`/race`** - Opens the main racing menu
- **`/help`** - Shows detailed instructions
- **`/tracks`** - Jump directly to track selection

## How to Race

### Step-by-Step Racing

#### 1. Track Selection

- Open the menu with `/race`
- Click "Start Racing"
- Choose an available track (green = available, red = taken)
- You'll be teleported to your starting position

#### 2. Preparation

- **Get a Minecart** - Place it on the starting rail
- **Climb In** - Right-click (or tap) to enter the minecart
- **Wait for Others** - Give friends time to choose their tracks
- **Click "I'm Ready!"** - When the dialog appears

#### 3. The Countdown

- Watch the screen for the countdown: **3... 2... 1... GO!**
- Listen for the sound effects
- When you see "GO!", you're off!

#### 4. During the Race

- **Stay on Track** - Don't jump out of your minecart
- **Enjoy the Ride** - Powered rails keep you moving
- **Watch for Features** - Loops and drops are automatic
- **Race to the Finish** - First one across wins!

#### 5. Finishing

- **Cross the Finish Line** - You'll see a victory dialog
- **View Your Time** - See how fast you completed the race
- **Race Again** - Choose to go another round
- **Challenge Friends** - Compare times and compete!

## Tips and Strategies

### Beginner Tips

1. **Don't Panic** - The track is automated; just enjoy the ride
2. **Stay Seated** - Jumping out of your minecart disqualifies you
3. **Practice Solo** - Try the track alone before racing friends
4. **Learn the Layout** - Memorize where loops and drops occur
5. **Use Commands** - Type `/race` anytime to restart

### Advanced Strategies

1. **Perfect Start** - Get in your minecart quickly when countdown starts
2. **Momentum Management** - Lean forward (W key) through loops
3. **Drop Technique** - Stay centered during the big drop
4. **Finish Strong** - Don't slow down near the end
5. **Track Choice** - Some players prefer certain colors for visibility

### Multiplayer Tips

1. **Coordinate Start** - Make sure all players are ready
2. **Voice Chat** - Use Discord or party chat for more fun
3. **Spectate** - Finished players can watch others
4. **Tournament Mode** - Run multiple heats and track times
5. **Team Races** - Compete in teams for combined times

## Game Modes

### Standard Race

- **Players:** 2-5
- **Goal:** First to finish wins
- **Rules:** Stay on your track, no jumping out

### Time Trial

- **Players:** 1
- **Goal:** Beat your personal best time
- **Rules:** Race alone, focus on speed

### Tournament

- **Players:** 4-5
- **Goal:** Best overall time across multiple races
- **Rules:** Everyone races multiple times, lowest average wins

### Relay Race

- **Players:** 4-10 (teams of 2)
- **Goal:** Team with fastest combined time wins
- **Rules:** One teammate races, then the other

## Scoring and Timing

### How Timing Works

- **Start Time** - Recorded when countdown reaches "GO!"
- **Finish Time** - Recorded when you cross the finish line
- **Race Duration** - Displayed in the victory dialog
- **Personal Best** - Track your fastest time

### Victory Conditions

- **First Place** - First player to cross the finish line
- **Time Trial** - Fastest completion time
- **Tournament** - Lowest average time across all races

## Common Scenarios

### "I Fell Off the Track!"

- Use `/race` to return to the menu
- Select your track again
- Wait for the next race to start

### "The Dialog Won't Open"

- Make sure you're not in the chat window
- Close any open menus
- Try typing `/race` again
- Move closer to the race area

### "I Want to Change Tracks"

- Finish your current race or exit your minecart
- Type `/tracks` to open track selection
- Choose a different available track

### "How Do I Spectate?"

- Finish your race first
- Fly or teleport to watch other players
- Use F5 (third-person view) for better angles

## Troubleshooting Gameplay Issues

### Minecart Won't Move

- Check that powered rails are placed correctly
- Ensure redstone blocks are under the rails
- Try placing a new minecart

### Can't See Other Players

- Make sure everyone has the behavior pack installed
- Check that you're in the same world
- Verify multiplayer settings are enabled

### Dialog Appears Multiple Times

- This is normal when respawning
- Just close extra dialogs
- Type `/race` to open fresh menu
